How much should you eat?

A simple daily guide for a healthy adult.

Almonds

5–8 pieces

Soak overnight for easier digestion

Walnuts

2–3 pieces

Good for brain and heart

Cashews

4–5 pieces

Rich in magnesium

Pistachios

10–15 pieces

Light, protein-rich snack

Raisins

1 tbsp

Natural sweetness and iron

Apricots

2–3 pieces

Vitamin A and fibre

Dry fruits are calorie-dense, so a small handful (about 30 g) a day is enough. If you have allergies or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
